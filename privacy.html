Privacy policy

CornerPin for Canva · Last updated 29 October 2026

CornerPin is an app that runs inside Canva. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it. In short: the app doesn't collect, store or share any of your information.

What the app accesses

  • Images in your design. When you edit an image, the app reads it from your Canva account through Canva's app platform, so it can show and warp it.
  • The current page of your design. The app reads the page's size and the position of the image you're editing, adds a temporary editing layer, and places the finished image.

What happens to your images

All image processing happens in your browser. The finished image is uploaded to your own Canva account (your Canva uploads), the same way any image you add to a design is. Your images are never sent to orailnoor or to any other server.

What the app doesn't do

  • It has no server of its own and no user accounts.
  • It doesn't use analytics, tracking, advertising or cookies.
  • It doesn't sell or share any information, because it doesn't collect any.

Canva

Your use of Canva, including the images you upload, is covered by Canva's privacy policy.
