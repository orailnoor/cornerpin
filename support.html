CornerPin help

Pin images onto any surface in real perspective

How to use it

  1. Add the image you want to place (for example, a screenshot) and the image you're placing it on (for example, a phone mockup) to your design.
  2. Select the screenshot, open CornerPin from the Apps panel, and click Edit perspective.
  3. Click the light purple area that appears over your page. The editor opens over the whole page.
  4. Drag the image onto the phone. Drag the four dots next to its corners onto the corners of the phone's screen.
  5. Optional: use Size, Corner radius (to match rounded screens) and the 3D sliders. Image visibility makes the image see-through while you line it up; it doesn't affect the result.
  6. Click Apply.

Troubleshooting

  • The editor doesn't open: make sure you click the purple area, not the spot where the original image was.
  • "An earlier edit on this page didn't finish": click Restore page to remove the leftover editing layer and show hidden images again.
  • The image can only move inside its own box: it's in a group or frame. Take it out of the group or frame, then try again.
  • Edit perspective is greyed out: select a single photo or image (not a graphic, video or several elements).
